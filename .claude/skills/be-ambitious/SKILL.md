# Three kinds of ambition (better, not bigger)

Start with what kind of project this is and who it is for. These questions
apply to code, designs, automation and writing alike.

- **Altitude: the goal beyond the goal.** Ask what this is for. "A task
  list" may really be "stop forgetting promises", which changes what
  matters: easy capture, reminders and trust.
- **Depth: how the best expert would do it.** Use evidence beyond Claude's
  opinion: official docs, a test, a measurement or a reader trying a draft.
  An expert checks assumptions and plans for failure. For hardware, that
  can mean checking the datasheet and calibrating a sensor.
- **Range: a method from another field.** A state machine from game
  design, a checklist from aviation, a prototype from product design, or
  an outline from storytelling. Name at least one, give it a real look or
  one line on why not.

Write the result as two or three lines at the top of `PLAN.md`. Keep the
scope the person asked for unless they agree to more.
